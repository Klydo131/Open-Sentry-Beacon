# Glossary

The words the app uses, and what each one means in it.

| Word | What it means in Hope Beacon |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | The Director's room: Approvals, People and Pairing, Materials, Mail, Safeguarding and Analytics. |
| **Admin Reports** | Reports, trials and the record. In every leader's Menu; a Guide or Explorer who is part of a case reaches it through Settings. Marked beta. |
| **Advanced settings** | A tick box that shows more choices on a screen that keeps them out of the way, such as minutes and platform notes on a Sabbath program. Nobody ever has to open it. |
| **Ambience** | Calming sounds made on the phone as they play, in the Music room's Listen folder: *Calm* and *White noise*. |
| **Announcement** | A notice pinned at the top of everybody's Home, with when it happens. |
| **Approvals** | Who is waiting to be let in, and the names Guides have put forward. The most important screen for a Director. |
| **Beta** | A part of the app that works but is still being finished. It says so beside its name. |
| **Blog** | Posts written for the church, in Home. Anybody may write one with **Write a post**. |
| **Classic** | The app's own look, and the default. Every other look is chosen beside it. |
| **Commission** | The idea behind the journey: an Explorer who has been walked with becomes a Guide, and walks with somebody else. |
| **Concert pitch** | The note A that the Tuner measures everything against, 440 Hz unless you change it. |
| **Conductor** | The Music room's metronome, which draws the conductor's hand for 2, 3, 4 or 6 beats. |
| **Desk** | A Guide's or leader's side panel: the date, what is waiting, a note, the pocket and the colour squares. A drawer on a phone. |
| **Director** | Runs the church's account: invites, approves, pairs, and reads safeguarding reports. |
| **Disapprove** | Switch an account off, keeping it. It can be undone. Compare *Delete*, which cannot. |
| **Evangelistic meetings** | A planner in the Office for a series of meetings, night by night, with a Word file, a picture and a post to share. |
| **Executive Director** | Oversees one or more churches, appoints Directors, and sees what a Director sees across all of them. |
| **Explorer** | Somebody the church is walking with. They have one Guide. |
| **Folder** | One part of a room. The button at the top of a room says which folder you are in; tap it to see the rest. |
| **Front door** | The first page of the app, before signing in. |
| **Frutiger Aero** | A family of five looks made of glass and light: Frutiger Eco, Dark Aero, Technozen, DORFic and Four Colors. |
| **Guide** | A church member who walks with a few Explorers. |
| **Guided walk** | The tutorial: a highlight that points at the next thing to tap and says what it is for. **Start a tutorial**, in Settings, Help. |
| **Head Executive Director** | The one account at the root of the church's authority. Nobody can suspend or remove it, so a church can never lock itself out. |
| **Home** | The church home: Notices, Blog, the Prayer wall, and for leaders the numbers. First in every Menu. |
| **Install** | Add the app to a home screen or dock so it opens like any other app. |
| **Journey** | The five stages an Explorer moves through at their own pace: Beginner, Connect, Care, Call and Cultivate. |
| **Lesson study** | A study a Guide writes once in the Office and sends to the people who need it. |
| **Look** | How the whole app is drawn: its colours and shapes. Chosen in Settings, General, Look, and kept on the device. |
| **Mail** | In the sample church, a mailbox showing what the app sends. In a church's own app, the invitations still open, for leaders. |
| **Menu** | The list of every room you have. The first of the three words along the bottom. |
| **MusicXML** | A file format for written music that MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale and Dorico all save. The Music room plays it. |
| **My Files** | Your own files, on this device. Never uploaded. |
| **My Journey** | An Explorer's own room: My Guide, Study, Church and Prayer. |
| **New** | A green mark on anybody who joined in the last seven days. |
| **Office** | The room for work rather than conversation: numbers, lesson studies, the Sabbath program, evangelistic meetings and reports. |
| **Pairing** | Putting an Explorer with a Guide. They can talk from that moment. |
| **People** | The second of the three words along the bottom: the people you walk with. |
| **Pieces** | The Music room's folder for the choir's music: clean scanned pages and scores that play. |
| **Pocket** | A place on the desk for a few links you use often. |
| **Post it** | Share a Sabbath program or a series of meetings inside the app, for the people you walk with or the whole church. |
| **Prayer wall** | Requests the church has been asked to pray for, in Home. Names are never shown. |
| **Progress report** | In the Office, Reports: how the Explorers are doing over a month or a quarter, with a Word file to hand in. |
| **Reduce motion** | A setting on your own phone or computer. Hope Beacon follows it everywhere and stops things moving. |
| **Report** | Telling the church's leaders about a message or a person. The person reported is never told who made it. |
| **Room** | One of the places in the Menu, such as Home, Music or the Office. |
| **Sabbath program** | A planner in the Office for a Sabbath's order of service. |
| **Safeguarding** | Where Directors read reports. The one place a leader may read part of a private conversation, and only the part reported. |
| **Sample church** | The invented church the app shows when it has no database behind it. Every picture in this guide is from it. |
| **Shelf** | The church's starter resources. An Explorer is shown eight first, opening with a Bible they can read on a phone. |
| **Stage** | Where an Explorer has reached on the journey. A note for the Guide, never a score, and never shown to the Explorer. |
| **Study Room** | An Explorer's own place to write while reading. Nobody else sees it. Marked beta. |
| **Talk** | The round button in the corner of every screen: your conversation. |
| **Team only** | A tick on part of an evangelistic meeting that keeps it out of the picture and anything posted. |
| **Text size** | How big the words are, chosen in Settings, General, and applied with **Apply**. |
| **This Sabbath** | The room that shows the program and meetings your church has posted. Opens with no signal once seen. |
| **Tuner** | The Music room's folder that names the note you sing or play and says whether it is flat or sharp. |
| **Vault** | The Listen folder's tab for your own music and video, saved on this device. |
