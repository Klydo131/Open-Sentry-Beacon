# Questions people ask

The questions that come up most, with short answers. Each answer names the
chapter that says more.

## Getting in

### How do I join?

Somebody at your church invites you. There is no public sign-up, and there
never will be: the invitation is the only way in. If you have not had one, ask
the person at your church who looks after the app.

### My invitation says it has expired, or has already been used.

A second invitation to the same address switches off the first one, and a link
works once. Use the **newest** email you were sent. If you are not sure which
that is, ask your church to press **Re-send**, and use only what arrives after
that.

### The password in my invitation does not work.

It works for three days after it was sent. It is three words and a number
joined by dashes, such as `harbor-acorn-river-48`: type the dashes too. After
three days, tap **Send me a link to set my password** on the sign-in page and
choose your own.

### Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Hope Beacon is a website that installs like an app. Open the link, then add
it to your home screen: in **Safari** on an iPhone or iPad, or in **Chrome** on
Android. *Getting it on your phone* has the steps with pictures.

### Which phones and computers does it work on?

Any current phone, tablet or computer with an up-to-date browser. *Which
platforms this runs on* separates what has been tested from what is expected to
work.

### It asked me to sign in again after I installed it.

That is normal, once. The installed copy keeps its own sign-in, and remembers
you after that.

## Using it day to day

### Where is the conversation with my Guide?

It is the round **Talk** button in the corner of every screen. A number on it
means your Guide has written.

### Can I take back a message I sent?

Yes. Tap your message, then **Edit** or **Delete**. An edited message is marked
*edited*, and a deleted one leaves a line saying a message was deleted, so the
other person is never left wondering whether they imagined it.

### How long can a voice message be?

Up to two minutes. It never sends on its own: tap the arrow to send, or the bin
to throw it away.

### I cannot see Reply, the reactions or the microphone.

Your church's database has not had the update that switches them on yet.
Everything else in the conversation works the same. Tell whoever looks after the
app; the companion guide explains the update.

### I cannot find a button somebody told me about.

Check which folder you are in. Tap the button at the top of the room: what you
want is almost always in another folder of the same room.

### Does it work with no signal?

Yes, for anything you have opened before. Rooms open, **This Sabbath** shows
the last program you saw, your music plays, and what you write waits on the
phone and goes up by itself when the signal comes back.

### How do I make the words bigger?

**Settings**, **General**, **Text size**: choose a size, check the preview,
then tap **Apply**.

### Can the app be dark?

Yes. In **Settings**, **General**, **Look**, choose **Focus** or **Dark Aero**.
*Settings, looks and reading comfort* shows every look side by side.

### How do I update the app?

You do not. It updates itself the next time it starts, with nothing to press.
If something looks out of place right after an update, close the app fully and
open it again.

### My new profile picture is not showing.

Close the app fully and open it again, once.

## Your privacy

### Can church leaders read my conversation?

No. A conversation belongs to the two people in it. The only exception is a
safeguarding report: if somebody reports a message, a Director may read the
part that was reported, and the app says so on the screen where a report is
made.

### If I ask the church to pray for me, will they see my name?

No. A request goes to your Guide alone unless you tick the box for the whole
church, and when the church prays for it **your name is never shown**.

### Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. My Files and the Music room are kept on the device you are holding and are
never uploaded. A new phone starts with them empty.

### Who can read my Study Room?

Nobody but you: not your Guide, not a Director, not the person who set up the
app. Your pages are kept in the church's database so they follow you to another
device, under rules the database itself enforces that let only you read them.

### Does the tuner record me?

No. It listens only after you tap **Start listening**, measures the sound on
the phone, and throws it away. It lets go of the microphone when you stop, when
you leave the Tuner, and when the phone locks.

### Where was my photo taken? Can others see that?

No. Photographs are made smaller and have their location removed before they
are sent in a conversation.

### Can I have a copy of everything the app holds about me?

Yes. **Profile**, then **A copy of your information**. No reason is needed and
nobody has to approve it. *Your information, and how it is protected* says what
is in the file, and the three things that are left out and why.

### Can I change my own role?

No, and nobody else can change their own either, including the Executive
Director. A role is chosen by the church when somebody is let in, and the
database itself refuses anybody who tries to change their own.

## For Guides

### How many Explorers can I walk with?

As many as your church has set. It starts at five, which is roughly how many
people one person can really walk with. A Director can raise it in the church's
settings; a Guide cannot raise their own.

### I advanced somebody's stage by mistake.

Tap **Undo, step back** beside it. It is recorded as a correction, and the
Explorer never sees their stage either way.

### Can I invite somebody myself?

You can **put a name forward** from the Office. A Director then invites them,
and they arrive already paired with you.

### My Sabbath program is not on my other phone.

In a church's own app, programs follow you to every device where you sign in,
once the phone that made them has had a signal. The line under **Saved** says
whether it has reached your account yet.

### An Explorer cannot see the program I posted.

It went to the people you walk with, and that Explorer walks with somebody
else; or it is still a draft in your blog. Post it again for **Everyone in the
church**.

## For Directors

### What is the most important screen?

**Admin**, then **Approvals**. An Explorer with no Guide has no app, so anything
waiting there is somebody being left alone.

### What is the difference between Disapprove and Delete?

**Disapprove** switches an account off and keeps it, and can be undone.
**Delete** removes the account, its messages and its pairings for good, and
frees the email address. Use Disapprove when you are looking into something,
and Delete when somebody has left.

### What should I look at each week?

Four questions, five minutes: is anybody waiting in **Approvals**; is anybody
unpaired; is any Guide at their limit while Explorers wait; and is there
anything in **Admin Reports**.

### Somebody reported a message. What do I do?

Open **Admin**, then **Safeguarding**. *Reports, trials and the record* explains
the whole process, and why the person reported is never told who reported them.

## About the app itself

### Who makes it, and who owns our data?

The app is open source, made by volunteers and shared under the GNU Affero
General Public License. Your church's copy runs on your church's own database,
so your church holds its members' information, not the people who wrote the
app.

### What does it cost?

The app itself is free. Running a church's own copy costs whatever the database
and the web host charge; *What it costs* has measured figures, and the
companion guide explains each service.

### Can we change it?

Yes. That is what open source means here. The companion guide, *Building Hope
Beacon*, starts from a first download and ends at a reviewed change.

### Something is wrong and it is not in this guide.

Use **Send feedback**, at the bottom of the front door or in **Settings**,
**Help**. A sentence is enough.
