# About this guide

This is the complete guide to **using** Hope Beacon: every room, every folder
and every role, in plain words, with a picture of each screen on a phone and on
a computer.

Its companion, *Building Hope Beacon*, is for the people who set the app up,
host it and change it. You do not need it to use the app.

## Who it is for

| If you are… | Start with | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **An Explorer**, somebody the church is walking with | *Getting it on your phone*, then *If you are an Explorer* | *The Study Room* and *The Music room* |
| **A Guide**, walking with Explorers | *If you are a Guide* | *Running it, week to week*, and your own screens in *Every screen, in pictures* |
| **A Director** or **Executive Director** | *The four roles* and *Onboarding a church* | *If you are a Director*, then all of Part IV |
| **The church office**, or whoever helps people get started | *Getting started* (Part II) | *If something looks wrong* and *Questions people ask* |
| **Deciding whether your church should use it** | *Hope Beacon in one look* | *What it costs*, *Your information* and *Showing it to a church* |

Nobody needs to read it all. Every chapter stands on its own, and the
**Contents** page numbers are clickable.

## How it is laid out

- **Part I, Welcome.** What the app is and the idea behind it.
- **Part II, Getting started.** Installing it, signing in, finding your way,
  and making it comfortable to read.
- **Part III, A tour, role by role.** The app as each person meets it.
- **Part IV, Running it, week to week.** The detail behind every task.
- **Part V, Every screen, in pictures.** An atlas of the whole app, role by
  role, a phone beside a computer.
- **Part VI, Help and reference.** When something looks wrong, common
  questions, your information, cost, a glossary and what changed.

## What the pictures show

Every picture is the real app, photographed on the day this edition was built.
None of it is drawn or mocked up.

The people in the pictures are the **sample church**: John Reyes (an Explorer),
Maria Santos (a Guide), Pastor Ramos (a Director) and Bishop Alonzo (an
Executive Director). They are invented. The sample church runs inside the
browser with no database at all, which is why a purple bar along the top says
*Tutorial* and *No database connected*. **No real member of any church appears
anywhere in this guide.**

Your church's own app looks the same, without that bar, and with your own
people in it.

> **GOOD TO KNOW** · You can open the sample church yourself
>
> When the app has no database behind it, its front door opens the sample
> church: press **Choose someone to be** and pick who to be. In Settings, the
> **Help** folder has **Start a tutorial**, a guided walk through your own part
> of the app. Nothing you do in either reaches a real person, so they are the
> safest way to practise anything in this guide before doing it for real.

## The words used for what you see

- A **room** is one of the places listed in the **Menu**: Home, Music, the
  Office, and so on.
- A **folder** is one part of a room. The button at the top of a room says
  which folder you are in, such as *My Guide, 1 of 4*. Tap it to see the rest.
- **Bold words** are what is written on the screen, so you can look for them:
  **Write a post**, **Apply**, **Start listening**.
- *The four roles* have a chapter of their own, and every other word the app uses
  is in the **Glossary** near the end.

The notes in coloured boxes mean:

> **NOTE** · Something worth knowing, but not a step.

> **GOOD TO KNOW** · A shortcut, or a way to make something easier.

> **IMPORTANT** · Read this before you do the step it is next to.

> **CAUTION** · This cannot be undone, or it affects somebody else.

## This edition

This edition was built from the app's own documents on the date on its cover.
The guide is generated from the same writing the project keeps on GitHub, and
the pictures are retaken each time, so a new edition always shows the app as it
is. If something here no longer matches what you see, the app has moved on:
tell the people who look after your church's copy, or use **Send feedback** in
the app.
