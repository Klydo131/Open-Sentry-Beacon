# Settings, looks and reading comfort

Everything that is about **this device and this account**, rather than about
the church, is in **Settings**: the last room in the Menu, under *You*. This
chapter goes through it folder by folder, then shows every look the app has,
on a phone and on a computer, so you can choose one before you try it.

## The folders in Settings

| Folder | Who has it | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **General** | Everybody | Installing the app, alerts, **Text size**, **Look**, and language |
| **Password** | Everybody, in a church's own app | Changing your password. First for anybody who arrived on a password sent by email |
| **Admin Reports** | Everybody, in a church's own app | Any report or case you are part of, so it is never out of reach |
| **Church** | Directors | The church's name, and its data |
| **Help** | Everybody | **Start a tutorial**, what is new, **Send feedback**, and where to find help |

The sample church has General, Church and Help; a church's own app adds
Password and Admin Reports, because those only mean something with real
accounts behind them.

## Making the words bigger

1. Open **Settings**. You are in **General**.
2. Under **Text size**, tap a larger size. The preview underneath shows it
   first, on a sample of real text.
3. When it reads well, tap **Apply**. Every screen follows, conversations
   included. **Keep Normal** leaves it as it was.

Text size belongs to the device, not to your account: a phone can have large
words while your computer stays at Normal. It is kept when the app updates.

> **GOOD TO KNOW** · For somebody who finds reading on a phone hard
>
> Choose the largest size, then the **Study** look (warm paper, dark ink), and
> hold the phone on its side in a conversation. Every screen is built to still
> fit at the largest size; if one does not, that is a fault worth reporting.

## Choosing a look

A **look** changes the colours and shapes of the whole app. It never changes
the rooms, the people, what anybody can see, or where any button is. The choice
is kept on this device only, and **Classic** always takes you back.

1. Open **Settings**, stay in **General**, and find **Look**.
2. Tap **Classic**, **Beacon**, **Study** or **Focus**.
3. Or tap the **Frutiger Aero** row to open five more, each with a picture and a
   line about it, and tap the one you like.

| Look | What it is like | A good choice for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic** | The app as it was first drawn: navy and gold, clear and plain | Everybody; it is the default |
| **Beacon** | Bright sky and blue accents, room cards easy to spot | A bright room, or people who like colour |
| **Study** | Warm paper and brown ink | Long reading, and tired eyes |
| **Focus** | A night sky: dark surfaces, pale blue accents | Evenings, and dim rooms |
| **Frutiger Eco** | Sky, water and leaves; clean and fresh | A light, cheerful look |
| **Dark Aero** | Deep blue glass and glowing light | Long, focused sessions in the evening |
| **Technozen** | Calm and minimal, soft sage, very little shine | People who find busy screens tiring |
| **DORFic** | Playful and futuristic, the glossiest of all | Younger groups, and fun |
| **Four Colors** | A bright colour for each room | Finding rooms by colour at a glance |

Every look is checked before each release: words on a button must still be
readable on the brightest part of its shine, and text must keep its contrast
on every card. If you find a place where it does not, use **Send feedback**.

## Your desk's colours

Guides and leaders have a **desk**: on a computer it is down the right-hand
side; on a phone it is a drawer, opened from the small cabinet on the right
edge. The colour squares at its top recolour the whole app, in every look:

- **The first square is the look's own colours.** Choose it to go back.
- **The others recolour the page, the cards, the desk and the buttons**, while
  keeping the look light or dark. Under Focus, *Warm Office* is a warm evening
  brown rather than cream, so nothing becomes hard to read.
- **Each look remembers its own choice**, on this device.

## Alerts

The **bell** at the top holds your alerts and their switch. Alerts are on by
default. Tapping one opens what it is about and marks it read.

To have them appear on your phone or computer like any other app's, tap
**Turn on device alerts** and allow it when the browser asks. One appears
straight away, so you can see it worked. After that, anything new reaches your
notification tray while Hope Beacon is open or installed.

> **NOTE** · When the app is completely closed
>
> Alerts reach a phone while the app is open, or installed and running. Alerts
> to a phone where the app is fully closed need a push service that each church
> sets up for itself; the companion guide explains it.

## Language

The app can be shown in English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian,
Tagalog and Cebuano. English is complete; the others translate the app's main
words, titles and buttons, and fall back to English for the longer sentences
until a native speaker has reviewed them. Choose yours in **General**.

## Movement, and reduce motion

Panels unfold from the button that opened them, and a new folder fades in. If
movement on a screen makes you dizzy, turn on **Reduce motion** in your phone's
or computer's own accessibility settings: Hope Beacon follows it everywhere, and
panels simply appear.

## Installing, from Settings

The **General** folder has the steps for installing the app on the device you
are holding, worked out for that device: Safari on an iPhone or iPad, Chrome on
Android, or a computer. They are written out in full in *Getting it on your
phone*.
