# How to use the Study Room

**For Explorers. Everything in here is yours.**

The Study Room is your own place to write while you read. It is not a chat, it
is not shared, and nobody is waiting for you to finish anything in it.

---

## Opening it, and leaving it

Tap **📖 Study Room** in My Rooms. It fills the whole screen, because it is a
room rather than a box on a page.

To leave, tap **← Hope Beacon** at the top left. That puts you back in the app
exactly where the rest of your journey is. Nothing is lost by leaving; there is
no Save button anywhere in this room and there does not need to be.

---

## The shelf

The room opens on your pages rather than on a page, the same way the Library
opens on books.

**Each row is one page** and shows its name, the first line of what you have
written on it, and when you last wrote in it. Tap a row to open it.

**The three places** are along the top on a phone and down the side on a bigger
screen:

| | |
|---|---|
| **All pages** | Everything you have, newest writing first |
| **Starred** | The ones you tap the star on |
| **Bin** | Pages you have put away |

**Search** looks at both the name and the words on the page, so you can find a
page by something you remember writing on it even if you never named it.

**+ New page** starts a fresh one and opens it straight away.

Your pages are grouped by when you last wrote in them: Today, Yesterday, Earlier
this week, This month, Older. That is usually how you are looking for them.

---

## Writing on a page

The first line is the **name of the page**. Type there to name it; the name is
what you will see on the shelf and in search. You can leave it blank and name it
later.

Below that is the page itself. Start typing.

### Type `/` for anything that is not a plain sentence

Press the `/` key on a line and a menu opens. This is the one thing worth
remembering, because everything else lives behind it:

- **Heading 1, 2, 3** for the parts of a study
- **Bulleted list** and **Numbered list**
- **To-do** for things to come back to, with a box you can tick
- **Quote** for a verse or a line from a book
- **Divider** to separate one day from the next
- **Table** for a comparison
- **Callout** for something you want to stand out
- **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Yesterday** to drop a date in

Start typing after the `/` to narrow the list, then press Enter.

### Select words to format them

Highlight any words and a small bar appears with **bold**, *italic*, underline,
strikethrough, inline code, a link, and text colour. The same bar can turn a
paragraph into a heading or a list.

**On a phone**, the bar sits above the on-screen keyboard while you are typing.

### Keyboard shortcuts, if you are on a computer

| | |
|---|---|
| Bold | Ctrl+B (Cmd+B on a Mac) |
| Italic | Ctrl+I |
| Underline | Ctrl+U |
| Link | Ctrl+K |
| Undo | Ctrl+Z |

## Looking after your pages

**Star** a page with the ☆ on its row and it appears in Starred. Tap again to
unstar it.

**Move to bin** takes a page off the shelf without destroying it. It waits in
the Bin until you decide.

**Put it back** returns a page from the Bin to All pages.

**Delete forever** lives only in the Bin, is the only red button in the room,
and asks you once before it does anything. After that the page is gone and
cannot be recovered.

---

## Saving, and what to do if it says it has not saved

Your writing saves itself as you go. There is nothing to press.

If something is wrong, the room tells you instead of pretending:

**"Not saved yet"** in red means your writing has not reached the church's
database. Keep the page open. It keeps trying, and what you have written is
still on the screen. When it gets through, the message disappears on its own.
If it stays for more than a few minutes, it is almost always the connection.

**"Your study room did not answer"** means the app could not reach your pages at
all when you opened it. Nothing has been lost; your pages are in the database
and this device simply could not get to them. Tap **Try again**.

**"Your saved pages are taking a while to arrive"** means the room opened before
everything had come back. What is on the screen is yours to read; give it a
moment before you rely on new writing being kept.

---

## Who can see what you write

**Nobody.** Not your Guide, not a Director, not the person who set up the app.

Your Guide can see **that** you have been working in your study room and **when**
you last did. They cannot see the pages, the names of the pages, or a single
word you have written. That is deliberate and it is enforced by the database
rather than by a setting anybody can change.

---

## What is not in the room yet

Said plainly, so you are not looking for something that is not there:

- **Photos.** You cannot put a picture of your Bible page in yet. The room has
  nowhere permanent to keep pictures, and adding it before that would mean your
  photo disappearing the next time you opened the room.
- **The whiteboard.** AFFiNE's infinite canvas is not switched on here.
- **Coloured code.** The code block is left out because the highlighting it
  needs cannot run inside this app's security rules.

Each of these is a decision that can be reversed. If you want one, say so.

---

## If you get stuck

Anything the room cannot do, it says on the screen. If a message here does not
match what you are seeing, that is worth reporting: a screenshot of the room
with the message in it is the fastest way to get it fixed.
